Examination
• The examination of the course includes
    • TEN1: an oral exam
    • INL1: to assignments
        • an assignment connected to the seminars
        • another assignment in the format of a reflection
          essay in the end of the course.
TEN1 (2 ECTS)
• An individual examination
    • But, simultaneously taken by three students
• The students will self-assign themselves for provided time slots on
    • 3 December, 4 December, 7 December, 8 December or 9 December.
• The format
    • Intro
    • Quiz: a mix of multiple-choice and open-ended questions on paper
    • Reflection: follow-up questions for each student to reflect deeper on their answers
• Content
    • Theory and practice
    • Tip: Attend the lectures! The open-ended questions will be related to what is discussed in
      the lectures
INL1 (Two assigments)
• Part 1, group work
   • Each team (of 4-5 students) will choose one of the research questions
     provided in the course and write a short report (approx. 1000 words) and
     present their findings in one the seminars.
   • The research topics that students can choose will be announced soon and will
     target both theory and practice parts of the course.


• Part 2, individual reflection:
   • In the end of the course, each students will write a self-reflection report
     (approx. 500), covering their critical and reflexive learning process in the
     course.
