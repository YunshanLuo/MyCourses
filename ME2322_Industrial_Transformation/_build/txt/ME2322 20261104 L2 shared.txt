How do define these three levels?

•   What is a “sociotechnical landscape”?
    • Broader macro-political, macro-economic and
      normative/cultural patterns

•   What is a “sociotechnical regime”?
    • the interlinked mix of technologies, infrastructures,
      organizations, markets, regulations, and user practices
      that together deliver societal functions


•   What is a “technological niche”?
    • Micro-level formations
      – where radical novelties emerge
      – where new business models experimented

Technological Innovation Systems




Technological innovation systems are socio-technical
systems focused on the development, diffusion and use of a
particular technology (in terms of knowledge, product or both)
(Bergek et al 2008)
