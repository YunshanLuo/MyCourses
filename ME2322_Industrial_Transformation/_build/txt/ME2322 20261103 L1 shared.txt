• After a long phase of experimentation and competition – a dominant design of an automobile
  has emerged, standing out from steam and electric vehicles, and forcing several companies exit
  the market.
• The dominant design: all-steel, closed-body automobile with a petroleum-fueled internal
  combustion engine (Utterback, 1996)

intelligence (AI)

• AI as functions of machines, robots, software,
  etc. that mimic cognitive roles associated with
  the human mind, typically decision-making,
  self-learning and problem solving (Russell and
  Norvig, 1995)
• More-narrowly defined areas of AI: machine
  learning (Jordan and Mitchell, 2015), neural
  networks (Gurney, 1997), and large language
  models (Shanahan, 2024) etc.
