Assignment instructions (INL1)
• Form a group of 4-5 people.
• Attend all guest lectures this and next week, take notes, make questions and pay attention!
  Your case lies within these!
• Choose one of the four topics from those sessions: Food, Energy, Healthcare, LLMs...
• Identify the phenomenon or problem within that topic you want to target (guided by the
  research questions provided in the course).
• Choose the theoretical framework that best fits to answer the RQ and analyse your case.
  Make sure to be able to argument why that framework and not another!
• Choose your “data” from academic articles, newspaper articles, company websites, guest
  lectures, etc. described on Canvas.
• Analyse and write a report of aprox. 1000-1500 words (structure can be found on Canvas).
• Make sure to submit the report by noon on 24 th of November!
! Please notice !
• When choosing to analyse data through a specific framework or theory, it is important to
  first describe it, drawing on the relevant literature as part of your theoretical/conceptual
  framework, or as an analytical lens in your methods section. It is not sufficient to mention a
  theory by name and use it in the analysis; you must reference the authors and works that
  shaped that framework.


• Similarly, remember to reference and cite any statements that do not come from your own
  analysis but from existing literature.


• For this assignment in particular (and for the exam), the goal is not to review literature but to
  apply the theories and frameworks covered in the course.
Seminar instructions
• BEFORE THE SEMINAR: Prepare a 7-min presentation based on your report.
• DURING THE SEMINAR:
   • Spread out in different tables (do not sit with anyone from your group).
   • Within the people in your table, each has 7 minutes to present and 8 minutes to receive feeback
     from the rest. That is: you are both a presenter and a listener!
   • Think of a take-away from your discussions that you will present at the very end of the seminar
     time.
   • Take notes from the feedback you receive! This is necessary for the reflection in Part 2 (due on
     January the 8th).
