## 2. Team meeting #1 agenda (30 min)

Do this in one sitting, then commit.

**1. Theme (5 min)** — pick one from the four, or a combination.

**2. Concept (10 min)** — which lecture's theory? This drives everything downstream. Look at the paper list for that lecture; you'll need to reference those authors anyway.

**3. Case (10 min)** — this is the hard part. Work through:
- What exactly is changing, and where?
- Can you find press coverage of it in all three papers?
- Is it too broad? (Almost certainly yes — narrow twice.)
- Write the case as one sentence: *"[Phenomenon] in [bounded setting], [timeframe]"*

**4. RQ (5 min)** — draft it. *"How does [concept] explain [outcome] in [case]?"* Refine by 19 Nov.

**5. Practicalities**
- Seminar group: A/B/C/D — pick one **everyone can attend**
- Who creates the shared doc? (KTH OneDrive or a shared Google Doc — not email attachments)
- Who creates the CABS account? (One is enough, but two people check the rankings)

## 7. Rehearsal protocol (23 Nov)

Everyone presents the **whole** deck, timed, once.

1. Set a 7:00 timer. Stop at 7:00 regardless.
2. Each person presents solo to the others
3. Listeners ask the two questions the presenter answered worst
4. Cut whatever caused an overrun — usually the theory slide or the case background

If anyone can't get through it in 7 minutes, the deck is too long, not the person too slow.
