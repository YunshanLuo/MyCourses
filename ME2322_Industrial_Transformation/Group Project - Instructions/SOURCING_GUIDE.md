# Sourcing Guide — ME2322 Group Project

> The evidence requirements are rigid and easy to trip on. This is the practical version.

---

## 1. Requirements at a glance

| Requirement | Amount | Constraint |
|---|---|---|
| Newspaper articles | **4–5** | From **SvD, DN, DI** — **at least one from each** |
| Journal articles | **4–5** | Journals ranked **2+ on CABS AJG** (2, 3, 4, 4\*) |
| Guest lecture | **≥ 1** | Must be **cited** in the report |
| Theory | **≥ 1** | Concept from L1–L4 |

That's roughly 9–11 sources total for a 1500-word report. Do not over-collect — pick deliberately and cite all of them.

## 2. Newspaper articles

### Access
**Mediearkivet (Retriever)** via KTH Library — this is the route the course names.

- KTHB database list → search "Mediearkivet" or "Retriever"
- Requires KTH login
- **For long-running cases, Runestone is usually more useful** — it has deeper Swedish press archives and better date filtering. Check KTHB's list for it.

### Selection criteria
- Interviews with industry experts
- Debate articles
- Related news reporting

Avoid pure press releases — they're company material, which belongs in the unstructured bucket.

### Practical notes
- **Search in Swedish.** English queries return almost nothing in these papers.
- **Balance across the three papers.** One each is the minimum; two from your case's most relevant paper is fine, but don't put four in one and one in another.
- **Record publication dates** — the report needs a defensible time window.
- **Save the full text as PDF**, not just the link. Mediearkivet links may not persist for your teammates or the grader.
- Note: **Dagens Industri** is paywalled outside institutional access, **SvD** partially, **DN** partially. Mediearkivet is the way through.

### Search term patterns that work
- *"AI" AND ("vård" OR "sjukvård")*
- *("livsmedel" OR "mat") AND ("hållbar" OR "omställning")*
- *("energi" OR "elmarknad") AND "AI"*
- *"LLM" OR "generativ AI"* AND *"konsult"*
- Add `debatt` to surface debate articles

## 3. Journal articles

### The CABS requirement

The **Academic Journal Guide** from the **Chartered Association of Business Schools (CABS)**.

- URL: https://charteredabs.org/academic-journal-guide
- **You must create an account to see the list.** Do this immediately — it's the kind of registration step that costs you a day.
- Rankings: 1, 2, 3, 4, 4\*. You need **2 or higher**.
- Some editions also flag journals as "under review." Treat those cautiously.

### Finding the right articles

Search at the **concept level**, not the case level. The course says: choose them at the intersection of theoretical concept and empirical case — but the practical reality is that 3 and 4\* journals rarely publish on a narrow Swedish niche case. Papers on the *mechanism* in a comparable setting are the right target.

### Journals worth checking (verify each against CABS — rankings shift between editions)

**Transitions / innovation studies**
- *Research Policy* — 4\*
- *Environmental Innovation and Societal Transitions* — 3
- *Technological Forecasting and Social Change* — 3
- *Journal of Cleaner Production* — 2–3
- *Energy Research & Social Science* — 3

**Sustainability / consumption**
- *Journal of Business Research* — 3
- *Business Strategy and the Environment* — 3
- *Journal of Consumer Research* — 4\*

**Innovation management / organisations**
- *Technovation* — 3
- *R&D Management* — 3
- *Organization Studies* — 4
- *Information and Organization* — 3 (relevant for LLM adoption)

> All of the course's own reference-list journals are a legitimate starting point — Bergek (2008), Markard (2012) and Karakaya (2018) are all in *Research Policy*, and Susur & Karakaya (2021) and Bennich (2025) in *Environmental Innovation and Societal Transitions*. Chasing their **citations and references** is the fastest route to an on-topic set.

### Search method

1. Pick your concept and write 3–4 keyword variants
2. Search KTH Library's Primo, or Google Scholar via KTH's proxy for full-text access
3. Skim the abstract for one that does analytics on the *mechanism*, not just describes a case
4. Look up the journal in CABS before committing
5. **Citation chasing** — take one good paper and check its reference list and its citing articles

## 5. Harvard style

KTH's guide: https://www.kth.se/en/biblioteket/skriva-referera/referera/harvard

**Multiple authors** (test yourself — this is the most common error):
> Bergek, A., Jacobsson, S., Carlsson, B., Lindmark, S. and Rickne, A. (2008).

Not "Bergek et al., 2008" in the reference list (that's only in-text). The reference list names all authors.

**In-text**
- Two authors: (Karakaya and Nuur, 2018)
- Three or more: (Bergek et al., 2008)
- Direct quote: (Diklev, 2026, para. 3)
